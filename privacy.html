Privacy Policy - Bullion Pi

Last Updated: June 2026

At Bullion Pi, we value your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data:

1. Data Collection

We do not collect personal banking data. We only store public crypto wallet addresses and internal database ledger transaction history necessary to operate the application.

2. Data Security

All internal transactions are processed through secured databases and encrypted connections. We never request your Pi Wallet Passphrase.
