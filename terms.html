2. Gold Index & Pi Valuation

The core mechanism of Bullion Pi links the value of 1 Pi to the global spot price of one ounce of gold bullion within the app's internal ecosystem. This ratio is a proprietary algorithmic calculation used exclusively for internal ledger clearings and peer-to-peer simulation within the platform. It does not bind or represent global commodity markets or official banking rates.

3. Account Architecture & Stablecoins

  • Public Account: Interacts directly with the decentralized Pi Network Blockchain for standard deposits and withdrawals.
  • Stablecoin Account: Accepts decentralized digital assets (such as USDT/USDC) via external blockchain networks.
  • Internal Ledger: A fast off-chain database system that allows users to instantly exchange and clear digital assets internally based on the platform's gold bullion index.
